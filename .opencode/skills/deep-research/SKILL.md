# Deep Research

Deliver an evidence-backed answer at the depth and in the format the user needs. Use `researcher` for focused multi-source questions; a comparison alone does not require this skill.

## Scope and completion

- Establish the question, audience, scope, and requested deliverable from the conversation. Clarify only ambiguities that would materially change the investigation.
- Research until the major questions are answered with traceable evidence and decision-relevant contradictions or gaps are resolved or explicitly bounded. Continue through synthesis and the requested artifact, rather than stopping after an outline or first draft.
- Choose depth by evidence and the user's needs. Fixed source counts, word counts, phases, and time estimates are not completion criteria.
- Deliver in the requested format; otherwise use a cited Markdown response. Create report files or additional formats when the task calls for them.
