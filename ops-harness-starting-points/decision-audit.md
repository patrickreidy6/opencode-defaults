## Coverage and evidence rules

The refreshed inventory contains **76 loose files, including 12 ZIP containers, plus 431 archive members: 507 source occurrences**. All archive members were enumerated and read as bytes. Nineteen members/loose files are byte-identical duplicate occurrences, chiefly the packaged and loose trade-file sources. The path set matches the existing source-inventory.json; matching catalog coverage does not establish preservation of decisions. Appendix A assigns every occurrence a review disposition.

Decision records, handovers, architecture and plans were read first. Product reports and their mapped examples were compared with the briefs; supporting code, data and manifests were screened for scope, semantics and validation limits. Screening a dictionary or code file is not an independent proof of every field or calculation. Archived engines/tests were not executed. Every reported numerical example is historical evidence or a proposed fixture, not a newly certified result.

Authority is kept separate from relevance:

- **Direct user statement:** wording was visible in an original user-authored note/email. This applies to the narrow research/critic and form-review ideas, not the entire plan around them.
- **Recorded user decision:** a retained decision record or current interview handoff attributes the choice to Patrick. D04/D05/D17 have this status; the inaccessible original chats prevent fresh transcript confirmation.
- **Assistant proposal:** useful design or implementation detail, never silently promoted to a decision.
- **Research evidence:** scoped findings, examples or mappings, not universal practice or implementation authorization.

“Missing” means absent as a substantive commitment or specific known issue in the starting briefs, even if a source link exists. “Weakened” means the topic survives with less force, scope or qualification. “Preserved” needs no new mandate. “Conflicting” needs reconciliation. “Explicitly superseded” requires an identified later correction or owner decision; dates and silence alone are not used to infer it.

The current package records the 13 November interview; that record is the authority for this comparison, not an independently inspected original transcript. The live sim-funds decision file was also checked: D04, D05 and D17 remain present, so the weakened wording is not explained by their deletion in the owning repository.
